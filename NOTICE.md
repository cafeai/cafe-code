## Generated Visualization Fluid Background

The generated-document fluid helper adapts the WebGL Fluid Simulation supplied
with the user's Flowstate HTML template. It preserves the solver, shader,
shading, colour and orbit treatment while bounding local work and resource
lifetime. Unused bloom resources, external dependencies and page-shell content
are not included. The full notice is also retained in the embedded helper.
